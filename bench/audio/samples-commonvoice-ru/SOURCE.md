Utterances from the Mozilla Common Voice 17.0 Russian test split, https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/
(mirrored at https://huggingface.co/datasets/fsicoli/common_voice_17_0), released under CC0 1.0.
Speaker IDs are replaced by short hashes. Converted to 16 kHz mono WAV by `bench/scripts/curate-russian.py`.
